## 1. The category truth

Buying a sex toy online still feels like a confession. Three fears ride to checkout: someone will
see, I will pick wrong, and the store itself thinks this is embarrassing.

- **Lovehoney:** "Up to 70% Off." Volume.
- **Adam and Eve:** "Trusted since 1971. Great prices, free gifts and discreet shipping." Tenure and coupons.
- **Amazon:** arrives next to the paper towels.
- **PinkCherry:** "America's highest rated adult sex toy retailer." A ratings claim.
- **Dame:** "Clinically studied. Actually proven." Clinical authority.
- **Maude:** "Modern intimate wellness essentials." Euphemism.

Quotes are each site's own meta description or the 2026-08-26 teardown capture; recapture before
quoting on camera.

Discretion is universal, so it differentiates nothing. They all sell hiding and leave the shame.

## 2. The one idea

**The door doesn't have to stay closed.**

It is the only candidate that names the enemy, shame, rather than a feature, and every competitor
leaves that enemy standing. It is the owner's own sentence (charter line 21, 2026-08-22): "The door doesn't
have to stay closed, and we don't have to hide in the dark to use sex toys." And it contains our discretion rather than contradicting it: you
can close the door for privacy any time you like; you just never have to close it out of shame.

**Guardrail:** the door is shame, not privacy; no clip implies telling anyone anything.

## 4. The audience and the moment

**The first 1,000:** adults 25 to 44 who have wanted to buy and have not, or bought one cheap thing
and want better. Solo first, couples second. Evidence is thin (three customers, single-item baskets
of $28 to $82). They need permission more than education.

**The moment:** late evening, couch or bed, sound off, Reels or X. Not shopping. The clip is the
whole storefront.

**One clip:** permission in two seconds, one true fact, the temptation at the close.

**Voice brief for on-camera product talk:**

3. Porn-copy is a breathy read, a leer into the lens, or a body narrated mid-act. The cast want

6. Jokes land on the awkwardness, never on the customer or a safety fact.
7. Hold it like something you're glad to show a friend across a table; say it the way you'd say it
   at brunch.
8. The ear test: say the line to a friend across a table. If your voice drops on the noun, the line
   is timid. If you'd be embarrassed for the friend, it's porn-copy. If the friend could have read
   it off the box, it's robotic.

## 8. The signature and the frame system

**The signature:** every clip opens tight on a real adult already holding the product and
speaking, cuts wide on the first line to reveal a daylit room, and ends on a held look, then quiet
and the product alone on its ground.

- **Opening.** First word by 0.25 s; first frame per `video-realism-recipe.md` §1 to §4.
- **The hold.** "Showing you" (recipe §3): chest-up to mid-torso, eyes at about 30% of frame
  height, sternum at about 60%. Product top edge at least half a face-width below the chin, never
  crossing the jaw or mouth line; lowest point above y 1320 (69%), clear of the caption band;
  roughly 250 to 400 px tall. Whole hand visible, the other hand resting; the product never gestures.
- **Close.** A 0.6 s held look, then a hard cut to the 1.2 s **closing card**: bare packshot
  (archetype B) on the clip's ground, ink wordmark, one ♥ (color open, §12). No CTA text or price.
- **Marks.** Wordmark on the card only, no corner logo. Platform AI flag plus a burned-in
  `AI-GENERATED` kicker: JetBrains Mono 500, 28 px minimum on a 1080-wide frame, tracking 0.12em,
  ink on a paper pill at 85%, radius 8 (`--radius-sm`). Smaller smears in Reels compression, and
  an unreadable label is not a disclosure.
- **Captions** (post pass, word-timed). DM Sans 600, 54 px, paper with a 2 px ink stroke at 55%;
  no boxes or emoji; 2 lines of 26 characters at most. Band y 1380 to 1500 (72% to 78%). Clear:
  top 250 px, bottom 420 px, right 150 px.
- **Color and grade.** One ground per clip and card: coral-soft `#FFE6DD`, plum-soft
  `#F3E8FB`, or linen as paper-3 `#F4F3F1`. Neutral grade, 5200 to 5600 K, no LUT, glow or vignette.
- **Aspects.** 4:5 and 1:1 crop the uncaptioned master (1:1 is y 294 to 1374). Marks composite
  per aspect; safe-area percents apply to the crop; label at 6% width, 14% height.
- **Sound.** Instrumental bed, warm and unhurried, 84 to 96 BPM, ducked 20 dB under speech, cut on the last syllable. No sting. Hold and card carry the clip's
  own noise floor looped from a speech gap, or pink noise at -60 dBFS: -70 to -50 LUFS-S, never
  digital silence. The quiet is the logo.
- **Rhythm.** Cap 30 s, aim 15 to 25: plate, silent insert, plate. The 3 to 5 s insert follows its
  fact's line, never in the first 2 s or last 3 s.

## 9. The polish standard (render gate, pass/fail)

1. Renders at least 720x1280; master 1080x1920, H.264, 30 fps.
2. -14 LUFS ±1 integrated; true peak -1.5 dBTP or lower; LRA 11 or less.
3. A caption on every spoken phrase, in the band, clear of the product's bounding box.
4. AI label burned in per §8; platform AI flag set.
5. No text other than the manufacturer's mark on the body of a product we stock; no barcodes,
   cartons or labels; no captions or invented words in generated frames (doctrine §4 case 1, owner
   ruling 2026-09-19).
6. A shot is a source segment: at most 3 per 30 s, each 2.5 s or longer, every cut in a speech gap
   of 80 ms or more, cuts only. A reframe is a crop change inside one shot, lands in a gap of 80 ms
   or more, at most one per shot, and counts against neither cap.
7. Product legible by 2.0 s, roughly 250 to 400 px tall, above y 1320, matching the packshot.
8. One window key: one catchlight per eye, no second hard shadow.
9. Real skin, no waxy smoothing, unambiguous adulthood.
10. One hand, one job: five fingers, whole hand visible, holding hand still.
11. Product top edge at least half a face-width below the chin; nothing crosses the jaw or mouth line. Lip sync is judged at owner review until a
    SyncNet-class check lands (§13).
12. Ground on the lock and matched on the card (on-skin inserts exempt).
13. Grade: 10th-percentile luma 24 or more; under 1% clipped highlights outside the window.
14. No price, percent-off, countdown or CTA text on screen; wordmark and ♥ on the card only.
15. Bed cuts on the last syllable; hold and card at -70 to -50 LUFS-S, never digital silence.

Any fail is re-rendered.
