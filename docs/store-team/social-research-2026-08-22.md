## 1. Formats that are working

- **Platform data (Metricool, 24.36M posts, Jan 2025 to Jan 2026, published 2026-06-16).** Reels
  earn 4x+ the interactions of single images, average watch time 8.5s. Carousels earn about 9x the
  saves of single images and now slightly beat Reels on average views. Single images are losing
  reach (down 22%) and engagement (down 46%) year on year. Posts with a question get 36.7% more
  comments; comment prompts get 203% more. https://metricool.com/press-release-instagram-study-2026/
- **Carousel shapes trending in 2026** (whaaat.ai): contrast and split carousels, "WTF is…?"
  explainers, niche red-flag lists, "advice I needed at [age]", hold-and-scroll reveals,
  choose-your-path trees, anti-aesthetic camera-roll dumps. All map onto this category: "WTF is air
  pulsation?", "Red flags your lube is wrong for you", "Advice I needed at 25 about the gap",
  "Choose your path: solo tonight / together tonight".
  https://www.whaaat.ai/blog/instagram-carousel-trends-2026-12-formats-blowing-up-right-now
- **Reel trends as of 2026-08-21** (Later): "When you're a professional but you're also…",
  "Balance your expenses" (product weighed against text), "Sometimes you just need a little push"
  (a first-toy hesitation format almost verbatim), "Kinda chic to…", "Let me guess…", on-off toggle
  transitions (drawer closed / drawer open). The first 2-3 seconds decide distribution; completion
  rate is the dominant signal. https://later.com/blog/instagram-reels-trends/
- **What the category does.** Lifestyle-context Reels and discreet-unboxing content without
  explicitness are the standard move (Shopify guide). Lelo: design-led luxury and earned media.
  Lovehoney: humour and playful innuendo. Maude: intimacy as wellness basics, fashion-adjacent.
  Dame: advocacy as content (MTA lawsuit, "She's Coming" truck, Planned Parenthood partnership).
  Educators (Emily Morse, Shan Boodram) run short text statements plus polls.
- **Not verified:** POV, "things I wish I knew", "send this to your partner", confession and ASMR
  formats are documented platform-wide but could not be attributed to the named brand accounts.

## 2. Policy: what survives and what kills an account

- Meta's Sexual Solicitation standard removes "explicit or graphic detail" about genitals, arousal
  states, or sexual encounters; sexual metaphor and suggestive language about encounters is
  age-restricted (pulled from minors), not removed. Education and awareness contexts are allowed on
  paper. https://transparency.meta.com/policies/community-standards/sexual-solicitation/
- **Enforcement is harsher than the text.** Bellesa Boutique (700K followers, ten years of
  content) was permanently deleted around 2026-03-28 for "sexually explicit language in organic
  content"; per the company the trigger was the word "clitoris"; the appeal was denied and a
  replacement account was also deleted. Repro Uncensored counted 100+ LGBTQ+ and sexual-health
  accounts suspended in April 2026 under "sexual solicitation".
  https://xtramagazine.com/video/bellesa-instagram-ban-sexual-health-281447
  https://www.attitude.co.uk/news/instagram-suspended-100-lgbtq-and-sexual-health-accounts-522176/
- Sexual emoji (eggplant, peach), regional slang, and "sexual roles" are explicitly flaggable since
  the December 2020 rule change. https://www.vice.com/en/article/instagram-rules-censoring-sex-educators/
- **Shadowban mechanics.** Borderline content stays up but is dropped from Explore, Reels feed,
  Search, hashtag pages and suggestions; repeat offenders lose recommendation eligibility
  account-wide. Triggers: flagged hashtags, links to adult sites, spammy posting.
- **What survivors do.** Keep the account suggestive at most and push explicit detail to a neutral
  link-in-bio page; keep a backup account. Innuendo via absence (Lovehoney's "Not a Sex Toy"
  toothbrush-and-cucumber campaign, "we know a thing or two about restraints"). Dame's approved
  transit line, "Pleasure is a path to overall wellness. Dame is a path to pleasure", is the
  template for wellness register with desire subtext.
- **Algospeak does not help.** A 2025 study of 450 posts from 9 sex-ed accounts found no
  engagement difference between algospeak and plain posts, and it did not save Bellesa. The lever
  is what is literally shown and said, not spelling.
  https://rewirenewsgroup.com/2026/02/09/sex-educators-are-self-censoring-online-to-avoid-content-bans-does-it-work/

**Implication for xdipx, and the reason the 2026-08-22 ruling is "9 by implication":** the heat
lives in intent, framing, and imagery within §3.2a; the vocabulary fence is Meta's and is the line
between an age-restricted post and a deleted account.

## 4. What moves the mostly-female buyer, with sources

- **The orgasm gap (permission, self-knowledge).** Frederick et al. 2018, US national sample:
  heterosexual men 95% usually-always orgasm vs heterosexual women 65%; lesbian women 86%. Higher
  female orgasm rates correlated with more oral sex, longer sessions, and asking for what they
  want. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28213723/
- **Toys close it.** Menopause journal, Nov 2025 (3,000+ women 60+): near-always solo toy use was
  significantly associated with always orgasming; 38.7% use toys in partnered sex. Frontiers in
  Public Health, Apr 2026 (199 women): 52% solo use, about 30% partnered; partnered use associated
  with significantly higher satisfaction. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13149122/
- **Partner dynamics.** We-Vibe 2025 (429 Americans in 5+ year relationships): 45% own a toy,
  52.7% use with a partner, 82% of toy users satisfied vs 74.3% overall, 53.5% of toy users orgasm
  every time vs 46.4%, 62% have gifted or would gift a toy, 75.7% open to toys if the partner
  initiates, 87% of couples who talk about sex regularly orgasm every time vs 3.5% who do not.
  Barriers: stress 21%, time 20.5%, exhaustion 20%, kids 19%.
  https://finance.yahoo.com/news/vibe-reveals-key-sexual-satisfaction-130000360.html
- **Stress and sleep.** Lovehoney Twenty Twenty Sex Report (UK, 2026): 65% have sex for stress
  relief, 60% for sleep, about 20% of women (37% of Gen Z) for period pain; about 25% talk or would
  talk to AI about sex. https://mamabella.uk/lovehoney-2026-sex-trends/
- **Discretion and first-toy hesitation.** Market-report aggregators (cite loosely): 35-40% of
  potential buyers hesitate from embarrassment; about 60% say discreet packaging raises purchase
  likelihood; 72% prefer buying online; discretion drives 37% of repeat purchases.
