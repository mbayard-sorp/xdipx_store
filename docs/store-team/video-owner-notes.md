> the Writers Room retro when a theme appears in 2 scripts or 2 batches, and by `/video-room` live.
> Read by `episode-writer` and `script-doctor` at run start, before the first line is written or
> judged. Entries are never renumbered; a rule that is replaced gets a new entry that names the one
> it supersedes.
>
> Format: `N. (date) Owner said: '...'. Rule: .... Source: ...` (matches
> `.claude/commands/video-room.md`, §Write back, Standing lessons, the suggestion body).
