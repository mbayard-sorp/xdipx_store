# Imagery Owner Notes

> A numbered ledger with one standing rule per owner complaint, each quoting the owner. Appended by
> the social retro through an `instructions` row when a feedback reason appears on 2 assets across
> runs (`routine-social-daily.md` Step 2 item 7b), and from live owner sessions. Read by
> `social-art-director` and `media-manager` at run start, before the first brief is written or the
> first frame is generated. Entries are never renumbered; a rule that is replaced gets a new entry
> that names the one it supersedes.
>
> Format: `N. (date) Owner said: '...'. Rule: .... Source: ...` (the imagery twin of
> `docs/store-team/video-owner-notes.md`).

1. (2026-09-24) Owner said: 'exactly on message with bodyscapes'. Rule: https://www.instagram.com/p/DdrKEBUIKEt/ is a live positive precedent for the on-skin bodyscape treatment (`instagram-campaigns.md` §3.2c) under "The Orgasm Gap, Closed"; cite it when a brief needs a shape with live precedents. Source: ticket #11309 (owner live session 2026-09-24).
2. (2026-09-25) Owner said: 'You've got a lot of product drift on the rose images. The bodyscape is fine.'. Rule: bodyscape composition is landing, product identity drift is the defect. Shape, colour, finish and brand mark stay faithful to the bare product reference (`docs/design-doctrine.md` §4 item 3: hyperbole yes, misrepresentation no); scale exaggeration is licensed, identity drift is not. A render whose product does not match the reference is re-rendered, never submitted. Source: ticket #11487 (asset 675 vs ROMP 2.0 reference 97829B.jpg).
3. (2026-10-02) Owner said: 'We should stop doing forearm concept shots, they almost always don't make sense.'. Rule: `forearm` is retired as a body zone for on-skin bodyscape briefs on both Instagram and X, effective immediately. `social-art-director` must not propose it and `media-manager` must not fall back to it; a brief already carrying it is re-briefed to a different §3.2a/§3.2b mid-tier zone (inner-wrist, nape, behind-knee) before generation. Source: ticket #13120 (owner live chat 2026-10-02, following run 1199's zero-post-day report).
4. (2026-10-05) Owner said: 'what is missing is a story behind what is in the image ... we had decided ... use the writers room to craft scenes that have a narrative ... A cast member standing with a product in hand in what looks like an empty space is meaningless to our customers. Our customers need to relate and feel that they are excited to use the product in their own intimate space ... we should still be focused on bodyscapes that include the lingerie. It's OK to see cast members faces and bodies, but we need to show our customers that the sensation they are after, can be achieved with our products.'. Rule: every Instagram and X product frame is briefed from a Writers Room story line (`instagram-campaigns.md` §3.2g), set in an intimate space a customer would recognise as her own (her bed, her bath, her sofa) with lived-in detail in frame, and shot as a lingerie bodyscape in which the sensation the product sells reads on her face or her body. A cast member presenting a product to the lens, a product held up in an empty or generic room, and a frame with no moment in it are re-rendered by media-manager and REVISEd at the publish gate. The 2026-09-23 story rule had been written into §3.2c and never wired into `routine-social-daily.md`, `social-art-director.md` or `episode-writer.md`, so no run ever executed it; this entry and §3.2g are the fix. Reference failures: row 381 (Jade holding the La Nua bottle up to the lens on an empty bed) and row 384 (Marcus with the same bottle at a kitchen table). Source: owner `/all-hands` session 2026-10-05.
