# Ad owner notes

Standing rules distilled from the owner's ratings on Ad Studio ideas and creatives. The daily ads
routine (`docs/store-team/routine-ads-daily.md`) reads this file at the start of every pass. A reason
seen on two or more rated rows is promoted here as a numbered rule through an `instructions`
suggestion, and the agent-editor opens the PR. Nobody edits this file mid-run.

Format: `N. <rule>. Lever: <what it constrains>. Evidence: <idea or creative ids>. Added: <date>.`

1. A trust-signal mechanic is never the hook. No headline, subject line or lead line describes the
   plain box, what the statement reads, discreet shipping or returns. These lines need to be catchy
   marketing phrases. A discretion fact can still appear as one supporting line further down, as
   courtesy and never as secrecy. Lever: concept `statement-reads-xdipx` and any trust-signal line in
   any concept. Evidence: idea #6 (down, note "We don't need to advertise about our plain
   packaging"), idea #14 (note "We don't need to comment on what a statement reads in the subject"),
   creatives 26 and 27 (down), owner direction 2026-10-05 (#13588). Added: 2026-10-06.
2. Emma is never named in a headline or subject line. She can guide the landing page; the ad sells
   the product. Lever: concept `ask-emma` and every headline in every concept. Evidence: idea #15
   (note "We don't need to call out Emma in the subject"), creatives 28 and 29 (down). Added:
   2026-10-06.
3. Outside Search, a line has to earn the stop. The first run read as "boring and stale" (owner,
   2026-10-05). A headline names what this product does to the body or the moment, in words that
   only fit this product, at the top of the destination's register. Category labels and plain specs
   are for Search only, where they were hearted (ideas #1, #2, #3, #4). Lever: the hook formula on
   snap, adult, newsletter, owned and meta lanes. Positive precedents to write toward: idea #10 "Wild
   Rose: petal-soft silicone shaped like a mouth that doesn't stop.", #9 "Air pulsation finds the
   rhythm your tongue could never hold.", #16 "SONA 3: the slow climb, then the long fall.", #8
   "SONA 3. Cancel the three o'clock.", #7 "Sync 2: settle who holds the remote." Evidence: owner
   direction 2026-10-05 (#13588), the reasons on those ideas (strong-hook, more-like-this). Added:
   2026-10-06.
