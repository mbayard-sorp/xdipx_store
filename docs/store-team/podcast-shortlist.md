# Podcast Shortlist — sexual-wellness shows the reviewer draws from

> Owner-editable. The weekly `podcast-reviewer` routine picks the most recent unreviewed episode
> from this list (checking existing `podcastReviewBrief` docs to avoid repeats). Add or remove
> shows freely; the routine reads this file at run start. Shows were seeded by Claude on
> 2026-07-12 as credible, educator-led sexual-wellness podcasts — prune anything that doesn't fit
> the brand before the first run, and verify a show is still publishing before relying on it.

## Selection bar

A show belongs here when it is (a) hosted or regularly anchored by a credentialed educator,
therapist, or researcher, (b) sex-positive and body-safe in framing, consistent with
`docs/emma-voice.md`, and (c) general-audience educational, not explicit performance content.
Episodes with named-guest medical claims are reviewable, but claims get flagged in the brief,
never repeated as fact.

## Rotation rules

- Prefer the most recently published unreviewed episode across the whole list, not one show on
  repeat: never review the same show two weeks running while another show has a fresh episode.
- Skip episodes that are pure promotion, explicit performance, or guest-medical-advice heavy; note
  the skip and pick the next candidate.
- One brief per run. Quality over coverage.
